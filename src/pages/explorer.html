A closer look

Compare assemblies

Compare record metadata side by side. Assembly length is not a validated genome-size estimate, and N50 measures contiguity rather than overall quality. Annotation BUSCO values should be interpreted in the context of their lineage and annotation methods.

What do these measurements mean?
Assembly length
The total assembled sequence reported for that record. Duplicated sequence, haplotypes, missing regions, and other factors can affect it.
Contig and scaffold N50
Half the assembly’s sequence length is contained in contigs or scaffolds at least this long. Scaffolds can include gaps between contigs, so their N50 can be much larger.
GC content
The percentage of sequence bases reported as guanine or cytosine.
Annotation BUSCO complete
The proportion of expected conserved genes recovered as complete in the source annotation assessment. Missing values are unavailable, not zero. The explorer has not rerun BUSCO.

How to read this view

Cards show accepted-species counts and species with eligible matched records. Card tracks use the same 0–100% coverage scale; a tiny filled segment reflects sparse coverage. Groups without matches show a text label instead of repeated zero percentages. Select an order to inspect its families, or a family to open its species. Missing classifications remain available as unassigned groups.
