Source and scope

The first release uses the project’s uploaded crustacean_genomes(2).xlsx workbook, containing 410 NCBI assembly records. The original query and retrieval date are not recorded in the workbook. The dashboard is a frozen view of that input and does not query NCBI when a visitor opens the page. Accession links lead to the current NCBI records.

What the counts mean

  • Assembly records count input rows. GenBank and RefSeq pairs, alternate assemblies, and haplotypes are retained. These are not counts of independent biological genomes.
  • NCBI taxon IDs count distinct organism taxonomic identifiers. Unresolved taxa are retained. This is not a verified count of accepted species.
  • Families and higher groups retain the workbook’s labels, with surrounding whitespace trimmed. Synonyms and ranks have not been reconciled against an accepted taxonomic reference. For example, some source order labels may be nested within other source groups.
  • Assembly levels retain NCBI’s labels. “Chromosome” does not establish independently validated chromosome counts or completeness. “Complete Genome” does not by itself establish a nuclear assembly.

Records needing review

Records shorter than 100,000 bp receive a scope-review flag. This threshold is a screening aid, not proof of organellar origin, and does not detect every incomplete or non-nuclear assembly. Organism names containing “sp.”, “cf.”, or “aff.” receive an unresolved-taxon flag. All records are included by default; the explorer provides optional filters for these review flags.

Interpreting gaps

Remipedia, Cephalocarida, Branchiura, and Mystacocarida are included as comparison groups from the project’s review framework. The absence of a matching class label indicates no record in this input, not a verified absence from all public repositories. The comparison groups are not an exhaustive taxonomic backbone.

WoRMS diversity and accepted names

The species and genus exports are dated October 21, 2026. They contain 72,298 accepted species records and 10,717 accepted genus records. The species export contains 1,139 named families and 62 named orders; missing assignments remain explicit. Totals describe these exports, not a verified census of living species. Recent/fossil status is unspecified for 33,419 species. Environmental flags may overlap and are not used as exclusive categories.

Full scientific names are matched before normalizing parenthesized subgenus notation. Only unique candidates with a concordant higher class are used. Different family labels require WoRMS accepted-ID confirmation. Synonyms, spelling corrections, and alternative representations require exact-name WoRMS accepted-ID evidence or an explicit WoRMS NCBI-taxonomy cross-reference. No fuzzy spelling suggestions are accepted automatically.

A recorded subspecies may contribute to a species only through documented NCBI lineage evidence and a WoRMS-confirmed species in the supplied export, without a conflicting WoRMS target. Trinomials are not simply shortened. Accepted subspecies without an accepted species row in the export remain outside the denominator. Taxa with uncertain WoRMS status are not forced onto a species.

The reconciled snapshot contains {{matched_species}} accepted species with eligible records. Of 410 assembly records, 350 map to species in the export, 26 have unidentified source taxa, 15 resolve in WoRMS outside the species export, and 19 across three organism names need a verified reference match. Assembly review flags remain independent: all flagged assemblies are excluded from coverage. Repeated accessions resolving to one accepted AphiaID count once.

This is a matched share of the supplied export, not a complete estimate of all sequenced crustaceans. The assembly inventory has not been refreshed. “No matched record” means no eligible record matched in this snapshot and does not establish absence from public repositories. Reconciliation summary, full review report, and accession-level audit with evidence links document every outcome.

Family and genus groups retain their WoRMS lineage context to avoid merging identically named groups. Accepted genus records and distinct genus names in the species export differ; the headline genus total comes from the genus export, while Find a gap groups the species export. Missing classifications are retained as unassigned groups.

Reading the summary figures

The paired bars compare each class’s share of all accepted species in the export with its share of eligible matched species. Each series has its own denominator, and both use a 0–100% scale. These bars are not within-class sequencing percentages. The five richest classes are shown individually; “Other groups” combines the remainder, including unassigned classes.

The family figure counts {{families_reached}} of 1,139 named families with at least one eligible matched species. Unassigned families are excluded. The largest-gap figure ranks named families without an eligible match by accepted-species count. It does not establish global absence of genome resources. The order strip gives equal width to each named order, irrespective of species richness, and excludes unassigned orders. Card tracks show within-group matched-species coverage on a shared 0–100% scale.

GoaT genome-size summaries

The October 23, 2026 GoaT snapshot supplies 262 species-rank genome_size summaries with direct aggregation provenance. The query retrieves NCBI Pancrustacea descendants and excludes all Hexapoda descendants. Inferred ancestor/descendant values are excluded. Eleven species-rank records have unresolved names such as “sp.” and are retained as labeled taxa. The plotted summary value and original aggregation method, minimum, maximum and source count are retained in the download.

Direct aggregation provenance does not guarantee an independently measured experimental value. Each taxon links to GoaT for its original sources and methods. This chart covers a separate set of species from the assembly inventory and does not contribute to matched-species coverage. Genome size is not assembly span, and chromosome-scale assembly is not a cytogenetically validated chromosome count.

The chart was drawn for CrustGP from GoaT data. Please cite Challis et al. (2023), Genomes on a Tree and the underlying sources linked through GoaT when using these values. Download plotted summaries.

Updating the explorer

The repository separates source data, page content, styles, assets, and build scripts. Replacing the input CSV and rebuilding updates the dashboard without editing its charts. A workbook import script supports the current input format. The source README explains the update and GitHub Pages workflow.
